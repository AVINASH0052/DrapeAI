# Landing page overrides

> Overrides `../MASTER.md` for `web/` (sugatai.com).

## Positioning

**Modern + Indian + global**, product feels international; context is Indian life.

## Hero budget

- Brand wordmark in nav only (no eyebrow/tagline line in the hero)
- One typographic headline (`Your clothes.` + italic `Your look.`) with inline image capsules embedded in the lines
- One short lede with a rotating occasion word (`work / festivals / dates / travel / monsoon`)
- One primary CTA + secondary link + tiny microcopy
- No phone mock, no photo panel, no floating cards in the hero

## Motion

- Hero: staggered line reveals, capsule pan + hover expand, mouse parallax on capsules, rotating lede word
- Marquee band below the hero
- Scroll reveals (`[data-reveal]`) on every section, staggered per item
- Phone mock lives in the early-access section: slight tilt, idle float, cycling look card with dots
- All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Copy rules

- No dashes (em/en) anywhere in copy or meta tags; use commas, periods, or `·`
- Keep copy short: one sentence per section paragraph, one line per feature card

## CTAs

Early access → `mailto:hello@sugatai.com` until waitlist is wired.

## Phone mock copy

Everyday moments (`WEEKEND PLAN`, `CITY DAY`, `Today's look`, Mumbai weather), not festival-first labels.
